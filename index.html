PROMPT #07 · JEWELLERY STORE
Act as a senior web designer and front-end developer. Build a one-page website for [STORE NAME], a jewellery store in [CITY] known for [SPECIALITY, e.g. bridal gold and diamond sets].

Design direction: dark luxury. Background #120A0C, champagne-gold #E2B04A accents, ivory text. Headings in a wide modern display font, body in a clean sans-serif. Generous spacing, no clutter.

Hero: a slowly rotating 3D gold ring built with CSS 3D transforms (no images needed), a headline about heirloom craftsmanship, and two buttons: "Book a store visit" (WhatsApp link to [NUMBER]) and "View collections".

Sections: collections grid with hover tilt (Bridal, Daily wear, Men's, Silver) · why-us strip (BIS hallmark, certified diamonds, exchange policy) · testimonials (leave placeholders, do not invent reviews) · store map and timings · sticky WhatsApp button on mobile.

Motion: fade-up reveals on scroll, subtle parallax on the hero ring, respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Output: one self-contained HTML file with scoped CSS and JS that I can paste into a single Elementor HTML widget. Mobile-first, fast, no external libraries.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know coding?

No. You copy a prompt, fill in the business details and paste it into an AI tool. For Elementor, you paste the code it gives you into one HTML widget. You should be comfortable copying, pasting and making small text edits.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Claude (free or paid), Claude Code, Lovable, Bolt and ChatGPT. The Elementor prompts work best with Elementor Pro, but most concepts also work on Elementor Free.

What format is the kit in?

A downloadable PDF guide plus text files with every prompt, so you can copy them easily on your phone or laptop.

Can I use it for client projects?

Yes. Build as many websites as you like for yourself or your clients. You just can't resell or share the kit itself.

Will this guarantee I make money?

No. The kit gives you the concepts, prompts and pitch scripts. Getting clients depends on how much you practise and reach out to businesses.

Can I get a refund?

Because the kit is an instant digital download, all sales are final. That's why there's a full free prompt on this page: try it first and buy only if you like the result. If your download has a problem, contact us and we'll fix it.

What are the bonus app UI prompts?

Four prompts that design complete mobile apps (calorie tracker, AI trip planner, recipe app and dating app) with splash, onboarding, sign-in, main screens, paywall and profile, plus a design system. Paste one into Claude, Lovable or Bolt to get the full screen set as a clickable prototype. They're designs and front-end prototypes, not a finished app on the Play Store.
